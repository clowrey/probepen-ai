ProbePen-AI simulator

The pen's own firmware user interface, compiled to WebAssembly, running on simulated probe signals. Press its side buttons, change what the tips are touching, or drive it from the host app at the bottom, as a computer would over USB.

Press Capture to read samples over USB, or hold ASK on the pen.

ASK snapshots

Hold ASK on the pen: it sends its measurements and the samples on its screen. The real host app (host/probepen.py ask) saves each one as JSON for an AI model to explain.

Simulated parts: probe signals, the front end (divider, range switch, inverting gain stage, 31 MHz anti-alias filter, and a small zero offset for CAL to find), the AD9288 and its PIO/DMA capture, logic pod, DC-sense ADC, buttons and USB link. Everything on the screen and every USB reply comes from the firmware in firmware/src. No board has been built yet.
